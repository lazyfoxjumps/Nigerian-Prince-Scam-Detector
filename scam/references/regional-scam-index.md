# Regional Scam Index and Reporting Agencies

Use the user's intake location to (a) WebSearch the most active local scams, (b) plausibility-check the message against local institutions, and (c) route the user to the correct reporting agency.

Always WebSearch for the user's specific region in addition to using this index, since active campaigns rotate. This file is a starting baseline, not exhaustive.

---

## United States

**Active common scams (baseline)**: IRS impersonation, SSA impersonation, USPS / FedEx parcel SMS, Zelle "fraud department" callbacks, Medicare scams targeting seniors, romance scams via dating apps, pig butchering via "wrong number" texts, tech support pop-ups, gift card payment demands.

**Reporting**:
- FTC Consumer Sentinel: `https://reportfraud.ftc.gov`
- FBI IC3 (internet crime): `https://www.ic3.gov`
- AARP Fraud Watch (seniors): `https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud`
- Identity theft: `https://www.identitytheft.gov`
- State-level Attorney General consumer protection (varies).

**Plausibility checks**: Zelle is US-only. The IRS never demands gift cards. USPS does not text about package fees unless you opted in.

## United Kingdom

**Active common scams (baseline)**: HMRC tax refund / debt, Royal Mail / Evri parcel SMS, NHS COVID / vaccine survey, Amazon Prime renewal calls, "your bank's fraud team" callbacks, pig butchering, romance.

**Reporting**:
- Action Fraud: `https://www.actionfraud.police.uk`
- Take Five to Stop Fraud: `https://takefive-stopfraud.org.uk`
- FCA ScamSmart (investment scams): `https://www.fca.org.uk/scamsmart`
- Citizens Advice consumer helpline.

**Plausibility checks**: HMRC contacts by letter for serious matters, not SMS demands. Real Royal Mail rarely charges via SMS link. The NHS does not ask for payment.

## Australia

**Active common scams (baseline)**: ATO tax debt, AusPost parcel SMS, MyGov phishing, Hi Mum / Hi Dad WhatsApp scams, investment scams via Telegram, pig butchering.

**Reporting**:
- Scamwatch (ACCC): `https://www.scamwatch.gov.au`
- ReportCyber: `https://www.cyber.gov.au/report`
- IDCARE (identity support): `https://www.idcare.org`

**Plausibility checks**: ATO does not demand crypto or gift cards. MyGov never sends links via SMS for login.

## Canada

**Active common scams (baseline)**: CRA tax scams, Canada Post parcel SMS, Service Canada SIN fraud calls, romance, investment / pig butchering.

**Reporting**:
- Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC): `https://www.antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca`
- RCMP / local police for in-progress crime.
- Equifax / TransUnion Canada for credit fraud.

**Plausibility checks**: CRA does not demand crypto or gift cards. Service Canada does not threaten arrest.

## European Union (broad)

**Reporting**:
- Europol referral: `https://www.europol.europa.eu/report-a-crime`
- Country-specific national CERTs and consumer protection agencies.
- Germany: Bundesnetzagentur for SMS / call fraud, local Polizei online portal.
- France: Pharos: `https://www.internet-signalement.gouv.fr`, Cybermalveillance: `https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr`
- Netherlands: Fraudehelpdesk: `https://www.fraudehelpdesk.nl`
- Spain: INCIBE: `https://www.incibe.es`

## Nigeria

**Active common scams (baseline)**: bank impersonation calls, BVN harvesting, fake POS agent calls, romance scams targeting foreign victims (the global supply side, but also local victims), crypto investment.

**Reporting**:
- EFCC (Economic and Financial Crimes Commission): `https://www.efcc.gov.ng/report-a-crime`
- NCC (telecom-related): `https://www.ncc.gov.ng`
- Local Nigerian Police Force cybercrime unit.

## Indonesia

**Active common scams (baseline)**: BPJS / pajak (tax) impersonation via WhatsApp, fake delivery (J&T, JNE, SiCepat) APK file scams, undian / lottery scams, fake job offers via Telegram, love scams, crypto investment via Telegram groups, pig butchering, family-emergency calls.

**Reporting**:
- Polri Siber (cybercrime): `https://patrolisiber.id`
- Kominfo (Aduan Konten): `https://aduankonten.id`
- OJK (financial fraud): `https://konsumen.ojk.go.id`
- Cek Rekening (check if account is reported): `https://cekrekening.id`

**Plausibility checks**: Real BPJS, BCA, Mandiri, BRI do not send `.apk` files. Real pajak (DJP) does not WhatsApp demands.

## Generic fallback (region not listed)

If the user's country is not above:

1. WebSearch `<country> cybercrime reporting agency`.
2. WebSearch `<country> consumer protection agency scam`.
3. WebSearch `<country> bank fraud reporting`.
4. Also point to Interpol (`https://www.interpol.int`) for cross-border crime, and to any global resources relevant to the platform involved (Meta, Google, Telegram, the user's bank).

## Plausibility check checklist

For any scam claim, ask:

- Does the alleged sender (bank, agency, payment app, courier) actually operate in the user's country?
- Does the currency mentioned match the user's country?
- Does the alleged law / threat / fee match the user's jurisdiction?
- Is the language used the language a real local institution would use, at the right register?
- Are the contact methods (SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, gift cards) ones a real local institution would use?

A mismatch on any of these is a strong tell.
